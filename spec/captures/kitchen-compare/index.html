What was wrong, and what's done

The stair's open side (done)Should be a white cut stringer with dark treads and white risers over a papered spandrel wall, not a solid white block.
The stair's foot (done)Two or three bottom steps face the breakfast table between a short dark newel (by the foyer doorway) and a taller turned post, then a quarter turn into the flight along the wall. Ours starts straight at the doorway end.
Missing basement door (done, as a closed prop)Under the back stair's high end, in the same plane as the papered wall under the flight, beside where the flight runs into the ceiling (Marina 916 s; Kevin opens it in film 3607-3617). My first try put it on the side wall, which opens outdoors in our plan: wrong. The foot now has four steps (film 2528), so the flight runs high enough to leave full headroom over the door, as in Marina's.
The left side of the room (open)The kitchen search found our pier matches Marina's sideboard pier (her overhead, 1078-1116 s, film 10514), and her overhead and her interior views disagree on the room's rear wall length. Left as is until there's a measurement.
Finishes and fittings (partly done)Walls now cream and the floor quarry tile, as plain colours. Still to come: the small-floral paper, dark ceiling beams, cherry cabinets and blue tile counters, the hutch, the round table, the black fridge.
