McCallister House Fidelity

Each frame pairs a film still or a frame from Marina Coates's tour with our Unity render from the same camera. Drag across a frame to wipe between them: the reference is on the left, the build on the right. Arrow keys work too.

Renders are the latest batch captures on disk (date under each). They came from the builds of Oct 16–17, before the arch rebuild and the tree resize. Batch night renders read warmer than the editor does. Upstairs hall views come from paused work in progress.
