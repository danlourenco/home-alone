# Part 2 keyframes

Frames sampled every 45 seconds from the local Marina Coates Part 2 tour. The denser [detail sheet](detail-keyframes.jpg) samples every 20 seconds.

## Upper-floor constraints visible in the tour

- The labeled cutaway separates the girls' room, Kevin/Jeff's room, the stair hall, and the attic stair; preserve those as distinct zones rather than one generic upper hallway.
- The finished upper hall uses saturated green trim and walls with white wainscot/railings; the floral and dotted bedroom papers are room-specific.
- Kevin/Jeff's room reads as a long white-paneled bedroom with a green bunk, dark shelving, dresser, and a large window with green drapery.
- Buzz's room has the floral wall treatment, white lower trim, centered framed art, and a compact desk/window grouping.
- The attic is a timber-framed room with exposed rafters, vertical wood walls, a brick chimney/fireplace mass, beds, dressers, and small windows; the attic stair and chimney need to remain legible together.
- Construction and behind-the-scenes frames are evidence for set boundaries and sightlines, not measured finished-room dimensions.
