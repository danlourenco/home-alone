# Sheer curtain pass

Archived film stills `living-room-film.jpeg` and `party-film.jpeg` show translucent white sheers behind darker outer drapes. Marina's parents-room views support light window dressing in the suite. Sources previously researched: https://blog.selectblinds.com/selectblinds-review-christmas-edition-home-alone/ and local Part 2 tour.

`SurveyBuilder.AddFilmSheers()` creates 16 folded single-surface, two-sided translucent sections: living/dining front pairs, living rear pair, and the parents' east/front wing banks. They sit inside the glass and have no colliders or solid shadows. Buzz's green/roller dressing is unchanged. Kitchen and other unconfirmed window treatments are not inferred from these living-room images. No sheer fabric was added to every window indiscriminately.

This is a geometry/material pass, not simulated cloth or measured textile transmission. Fabric is uniform ivory with geometric pleats; weave, hems and detailed scattering remain a visual-quality refinement. `CheckFilmSheers()` validates count, translucency and absence of colliders. Day/night captures are under Assets/Captures/IntactHouseReference.
