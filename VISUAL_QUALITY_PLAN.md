3. Capture room reflection probes and test glass/brass/wood. Mirrors need appropriate room reflection rather than a sky fallback. Avoid adding many continuously updating probes.
4. Replace flat color-only material response with correctly scaled albedo, roughness and normal detail. Wallpaper should remain matte; wood, brass, fabric and painted trim must respond differently. Do not bake bright highlights into albedo.
5. Replace the most visible primitive furnishings with accurate proportions, rounded edges, joinery and fabric folds. Prioritize landing/foyer tables, chairs, balustrade and fixtures. Use Marina/film photographs to evaluate silhouettes before decorative detail.
6. Review at eye level in motion, day and night, and measure performance. Expand to living/dining rooms only after this benchmark holds.
