# room-upgrade: detail over the grey-box, checked from the user's eyes

The grey-box stays the walking and collision layer: its walk, seam, leak and peek gates must not change. The look is a separate
layer of models generated from the same spec, plus real textures, props and lights. Reference: the foyer (Oct 15),
`tools/house/hifi_foyer.py` (stair, trim), `tools/house/hifi_props.py` (props), `SpecGreybox.HiFiStair` / `TexMat`.

## Order of work (the user's eye ranks it: lighting > materials > models)
1. **Reference sheet first.** Pull Marina's frames and film stills of the room (the room's `art/home_alone/references/*` and
   `spec/captures/compare-marina/web/*-ref.jpg`). List every element: trim (baseboard, wainscot height and cap, chair/dado rail,
   raking rails on stair walls, crown, casings), fixtures (chandelier, sconces, switch plates), furniture, wallpaper, floor.
1b. **Openings audit (part of every room's first trial, not later):** each door and window of the room measured against Marina/film AND the nearest stock size (doors 28/30/32/36 x 80 in; stock sash sizes): built vs measured vs stock in the change record, and a real pack door/window where the other rooms have one. Oct 17: Buzz's door stayed 30 in through trial 1 although Marina's leaf measured 0.800 m (a stock 32 in); the user had to ask.
2. **Geometry from the spec, in Blender** (`/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P <script>`), one
   script per room, every position read from `spec/house_spec*.json` / `spec/dressing/*.json`; only profiles are authored.
3. **Unity:** instantiate the .glb under the right floor root, remap materials by name, strip its colliders, hide the grey-box AND
   dressing renderers it replaces (keep their colliders).
4. **Textures:** Poly Haven (CC0, user OK Oct 15) via `TexMat(name, id, tint, tile_m, smoothness)`: world-space triplanar, no UVs.
5. **Props:** Poly Haven models sized and placed by the dressing piece's measured footprint and height (`hifi_props.py`).
6. **Lighting** from the stills, then `Unity -projectPath <p> -executeMethod BakeLighting.Bake` WITHOUT `-batchmode` (bounce light; batch mode has no render loop, so no sky in the bake, and it refuses): every fixture placed where the frames show it (`tools/house/hifi_lights.py` models, `SpecGreybox.HiFiLights` places them with 2700 K point lights), checked from the frame's own view.

## Traps hit in the foyer (each cost the user a walk)
| Trap | Fix |
|---|---|
| Old pieces still drawn over the new model (grey-box `MainStair/*` AND `Dressing/*`: runner, handrail, newels, `bottom_step_bullnose`, tree boxes) | Hide by name across the whole scene (`FindObjectsByType`), list both parents; log what is hidden; probe the area for remaining renderers |
| Placed a wall panel at the plan's wall face; the visible face was 7 cm proud (a wall layer z 11.43-11.50 beside the flight) | Probe the renderers at the spot (`r.bounds.Intersects`) and use the face actually drawn; never assume the plan face |
| Z-fighting: a panel coplanar with an existing wall (under the landing); a raking wainscot 4 mm off the paper; the whole stair inside-out | Follow `.claude/skills/zfight-check`: overlays >= 1 cm proud, faces via `Part.face`, the build's z-fight gate at 0 and `glb_orientation.py` passing |
| Single-sided panels wound wrong = invisible (the spec -> Blender map is a rotation, not a reflection; `hifi_lib` reversed every face until Oct 16) | `Part.face(points, toward)` orients every flat face; never wind by hand |
| Per-step boxes for a stringer gave a stepped bottom edge ("this wall is a mess") | Build boards as one polygon: stepped top, straight edge parallel to the pitch |
| Baluster tops poked through the handrail (they ended at the grey-box's flat per-tread caps) | Derive dependent parts from the NEW geometry (top = rail line at that x minus the rail's half-depth) |
| A sweep path with a duplicate point (ZeroDivision) | Dedupe path points before sweeping |
| Floor read sky-blue (glossy wood, no reflection probe) | Smoothness <= 0.3 until the room has reflection probes |
| Wood looked grey: texture picked by name | Pick by measured mean albedo vs the colour sampled from Marina's frames, then look at it |
| A "fixed" view checked from a guessed camera; a camera inside geometry | `tools/house/note_views.py` renders each walk note from its own pose; close-up cameras stand in the open room |
| A missing piece the reference has (bullnose first step, raking wainscot) | Walk the reference sheet element by element before handing over |
| Wallpaper: Marina's paper is a different colourway from the film's | Use the film's (user, Oct 15): `tools/house/film_wallpaper.py` cuts one repeat from a frontal daylight still by 2-D autocorrelation (mask fixtures or cut the tile above them, never patch: patches show as blocks), flattens the light, and the loader tiles it at its own width and height (a square triplanar scale stretched it 9%) |
| Fixture placed from a misread frame (sconces first put on the foyer face beside the inner opening; the film has them on the vestibule's front wall by the sidelights) | Place each fixture from the frame where its wall's other features (door, sidelight, print) are all in view, and check that frame's view after building |
| Hanging fixture put in the wrong room (lantern in the vestibule; the film's chain shows against the foyer wall ABOVE the opening's head, so it hangs on the foyer side) | Read depth from occlusion: what passes in front of an opening's head is on the near side. Film shots "from inside the door" may be from outside it (film-1616 camera x 12.3): check a calibrated camera before inferring a second fixture; look for frames where the supposed fixture should show and does not (384, 3175, IMG_3843 ruled out a stair-well chandelier) |
| Flat, washed-out interior (no bounce light; HDRP ray tracing is DirectX 12 only, not on the Mac) | `BakeLighting.Bake` after each build: Adaptive Probe Volume over the house, lights Mixed (Baked Indirect), sky through the windows; ~20 s on the M3 |
| A wall's fittings judged by eye from a still ("sconces should be outside the vestibule") | Solve the film camera from known corners (`tools/house/film_solve_10248.py`: door leaf, sidelights, jamb edges), draw our outlines over the still, back-project the film's fittings, then render ours from that camera beside the still |
| Trim laid per wall ran behind doors, casings and the stair's skirt | Record trim runs while building walls and lay them last (`SpecGreybox.LayTrim`), sampled every 2 cm against the surfaces of what is on the wall; a baseboard 1 cm shallower than the skirt it meets |
| Full-metal brass read black (nothing bright to reflect) | Part-metallic brass (`BrassMat`) plus baked reflection probes (`BakeLighting`) |
| A casing placed in the dressing from one source while the wall was cut from another: a gap above it showed into the next room | Never place casing: mark the opening `cased` with its measured crown in the doors file; casing is generated from the cut, and the build's `Casing check` fails any casing in dressing or models |
| "Fixed" after a glance at a small render; up close the joint was still broken (user: "can you always verify please?") | Render the note's own pose (note_views.py) in the note's lighting (day or night), crop the flagged spot at full resolution next to the user's shot, and look before saying fixed |
| Lighting judged from batch renders (SpecGreybox.RenderViews): they don't apply the baked room reflection probes, so surfaces reflected the raw sky (a blue or grey wash that play mode doesn't have); four lighting "fixes" chased it (Oct 16) | Judge lighting in the live editor: clone the player camera (`Instantiate(Camera.main.gameObject)`), move the clone, render ~20 frames, `unity command capture_game_view --camera TmpCam`; never move the player (the user may be walking). Batch renders now switch the sky fallback off, so their colours are fair for geometry and finishes |
| A "fix" for something the user's own screenshot doesn't show | Check the user's screenshots and a live capture before changing lighting |
| Day rooms grey-blue, textures invisible (Oct 16): the day bake had no sky (batch mode) and the sun entered no window; leaks through the thin shell had been faking the fill | Bake windowed (it waits for the sky); prove a lighting input is in the bake by baking with it on and off and diffing the scenario's CellData; measure where sunlight lands with live raycasts toward the sun |
| A bright band along every ceiling: probes 1 m apart, a layer above each ceiling in sunlit air | BakeLighting invalidates every probe outside the room volumes (ProbeAdjustmentVolumes from the rooms); the envelope (SpecGreybox.Envelope) keeps the sun out of every joint, and the Envelope check proves it |
| Asset Store models (Oct 16): the fit stretched the wrong axes after a 90-degree turn (scale in local axes), a URP-authored pack imported with the error shader and its first HDRP copy was saved without textures and reused, the back/feet were placed on plan faces, not the drawn cap rail and carpet | List them in `spec/dressing/store_props.json`; `SpecGreybox.StoreProps` fits, turns (long side + flat back) and converts; the build's **Props check** must be 0 (fit 1 cm, back 0-3 cm and feet 1 cm against DRAWN surfaces, HDRP materials with maps). The packs are gitignored: the repo is public and store assets are licensed for use, not redistribution. The converted `*_HDRP.mat` live in the pack folders of the SCRATCH copy: `unity_pipeline.sh load` copies them back (they were missing once: magenta tables in the walk) |
| A batch render showed an old house (RenderViews defaulted to `Greybox_Upstairs`) | It now defaults to `House_WallGrid`; still pass `-scene` explicitly |
| The editor stopped answering for hours (Oct 17): `load` overwrote the open scene file and Unity raised its modal "scene changed on disk, reload?" dialog | `load` now switches the editor to an empty scene before copying; if commands time out with the screen awake, ask the user what dialog is showing |
| An editor command times out (the open editor is blocked by a dialog) | Copy the build into the project and commit anyway; ask the user to dismiss the dialog, then load |
| A wall seen edge-on in a film frame gave positions ALONG it 1 m off (foyer north wall in 1616: 2 px = 3 cm, and a fraction of a degree of yaw moved the hurricanes from 5.15 to 3.26) | Along a grazing wall keep the frontal source's positions (Marina); read only heights there, on the plane x = that position (`tools/house/foyer_props_measure.py`). Check a solved camera by projecting the build's own trim (wainscot cap) before trusting heights |
| `build_models.py` rebuilt other agents' stale models too (Oct 16) | Expected and safe when their sources are current; check the rewritten .glb sizes, and say so |
| Pictures cut from film stills (no licence) | Public-domain works matched by kind (`tools/house/foyer_textures.py`: Wikimedia Commons, licence kept in `spec/dressing/foyer_art.json`); Commons thumbnails only at standard widths (330/500/960), others answer HTTP 429 |
| Too many shadowed point lamps (Oct 16: 38 x 6 cube faces) -> HDRP shadow budget overflow -> blocky "random squares" and shadows that vanish when you move slightly (requests past `maxShadowRequests` are dropped; past the atlas, every map shrinks by a factor that changes as you pan) | Lamps use CACHED shadows (`SpecGreybox.Shadows.cs`: OnDemand, drawn on placement, redrawn by `ShadowRefresh` when first in view / camera in range / something moves near them), resolution by tier, HDRP.asset `cachedPunctualLightShadowAtlas` 8192. A new lamp: give it shadows and let `LimitShadows` pick it up; a new moving thing: call `ShadowRefresh.Near(bounds)` while it moves. Check with `ShadowAudit.Run` (dynamic tiles 0, atlas scale 1.000, 0 pending, 0 overlaps) |
| Judging cached shadows from batch renders | Batch `cam.Render()` bursts do not advance frames: cached maps came out unlit or blotched there. Use `ShadowAudit.PlayViews` (windowed Play in the scratch copy) or a live capture |
| Contact shadows on a lamp whose bulb sits in a non-casting shade near a wall | They march the depth buffer, which holds the shade: a dark blot of the shade's outline on the wall. Contact shadows only on lamps clear of surfaces (`ContactLamps`); HDRP serves at most 24 lights per frame |
| Balustrade rails that stop short (Oct 17 22:46 "bannisters don't connect": the grey-box gallery rails 4 cm apart at the corner, flight 2's rail ending 27 cm short in the air) | Every rail run ends at a newel's centre or in a wall, drawn in ONE model (hifi_foyer.py writes its runs to spec/captures/main_stair_rails.json); `tools/house/rail_joints.py` (preflight) fails any end not within 5 mm of a rail end, a newel or a wall |
| Trim stopping square at an uncased arch's jamb, its 3 cm end showing ("molding too thick", "ending abruptly"), and casing legs on a plain arch ("random vertical stripe") | Plain arches go in openings.py ARCH_MODELS (no legs); trim_faces.py `reveal_runs` carries baseboard and cap through the jamb and past the outside corner by the member's depth (a return) |

| Bought models (CGTrader, Oct 16): glTFast failed to import the .glb (a job-dependency error on FBX vertex colours); edited textures exported unedited (Blender `pack()` packs the file on disk); the bracts rendered black (a texture alpha link exported alphaMode BLEND) | `tools/house/purchased_plants.py`: strip colour attributes before export; write edited pixels into a NEW image before packing; link alpha only where the map really has cut-outs (the ficus leaves). Never commit them: output to the gitignored `Assets/Purchased/`, raw files in `sfm-work/purchased/`, the build falls back to the generated pieces |
| Another agent's `unity_pipeline.sh build` overwrote a scratch-only C# draft, so my renders showed their build | Land a draft in the repo as soon as it compiles clean in scratch; check the saved scene holds your objects (`grep m_Name:`) before rendering |
| Dark (night) / bright (day) dithered lines along the wall-ceiling joints and corners of the upstairs front rooms (Buzz trial 2, Oct 17): the overhang strip baked open to the sky with its probes invalidated, so probe cells beside the front walls had no valid probe and APV sampled the raw outdoor light; and "black boxes" by the windows were the reference scene's facade shutters standing inside the rooms | Diagnose with the clone camera and HDRP frame settings off one at a time (AdaptiveProbeVolume, SSAO, ShadowMaps) and a renderer probe at the pixels. Never invalidate probes inside a room (BakeLighting fails it); the strip bakes with its walls. SpecGreybox.Exterior removes any exterior part reaching into a room |
## Gate before the user sees it
1. Walk/seam/leak/peek gates unchanged and the z-fight gate at 0 (`spec/captures/walk-test-wallgrid.txt`); `glb_orientation.py` exits 0.
2. Every walk note in the room re-rendered from its own pose (note_views.py) and looked at next to the user's screenshot.
3. A render of each changed element from a camera in the open room, and a matched Marina/film view, looked at.
   Plus a CLOSE-UP at Kevin's eye (1.10 m, ~0.5 m away) of one joint of every kind the change touched (inside and outside corner, run-to-casing, run-to-stair), looked at full size. Oct 17: a cap-rail inside corner overshot by mm; renders from metres away and half-size thumbnails hid it, the user found it at Kevin's eye.
4. Load into the user's editor (memory load-scene-for-walk) and say what changed and what is still authored.
5. NEW SPACES (attic, basement, any room not yet built) and any LAYOUT decision (Oct 18: the attic trial was centred on its stair
   well, so the steps ran into the end wall and the bed faced the wrong way; the user's cutaway poster and the old reference model
   both showed the right arrangement and neither was read):
   - Read EVERY layout source before choosing one, and write a one-line agree/disagree table in the report: the film, the user's
     references (art/home_alone/references/poster/ cutaway: strong for the attic/upstairs layout), the old reference model
     (IntactHouseReference GLB: basement, attic, treehouse, yard; ~19% oversize, register it to our house before reading positions)
     and Marina. Film first, then the source the user named, then Marina; a single source's numbers (e.g. Marina's 476 ft2) are a
     check, not the layout.
   - `tools/house/stair_arrival.py` passes (preflight runs it): every stair arrives onto floor with a clear 0.90 m zone.
   - Arrival renders: first person at adult eye (1.60 m) from the top of every stair and each doorway into the space, and a plan-view
     render beside the layout source (poster/overhead) showing the hero pieces' ORIENTATION (bed head, stair direction), looked at.
