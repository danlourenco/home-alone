## Reconstruction sequence and acceptance

1. **Reference ledger:** select continuous moving-camera segments and overheads for each floor. Record timestamps, cuts, obstructions and repeated identifiable corners. Separate CG tour images from film/production images embedded in the same video.
2. **Recover relative geometry:** establish coordinate axes and consistent landmarks. Estimate camera poses/projection jointly with wall planes, doorway edges, window positions and floor elevations. Use normalized units initially; do not assume every door is80in or every bed a standard twin. A single frame's pixel ratios between objects at different depths are not physical ratios.
3. **Pilot on Buzz plus adjoining hall:** fit bed-wall width, room depth, doorway and furniture footprints from overhead plus multiple interior views. Use the hall/attic approach as connected constraints; do not treat the existing GLB coordinates as truth. Compare against at least one held-out view. Report alternate plausible fits where scale/depth is ambiguous.
4. **Reconstruct connected floor plans:** extend the same coordinates to adjacent rooms. Align floors through the main stair, landing, stair void and visible window relationships. Reproduce Marina before resolving any cross-floor/set inconsistency, and log each deliberate stitching compromise.
5. **Choose world scale:** relative proportions come first. Apply a documented scale assumption or a verified anchor afterward, with a sensitivity range. Prop dimensions are estimates unless independently identified/measured.
6. **Separate neutral Unity blockout:** build walls, openings, stairs and major furniture volumes from the recovered layout. Match saved reference camera views. No decorative detail used to conceal geometry differences.
7. **Validate two independent gates:** visual fit across views and physical traversal/headroom. Passing one does not imply the other. Only after these gates transfer reusable assets/materials, reconcile exterior, and resume lighting.
